We believe privacy should be simple and transparent. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why we collect it, and what you can do about it — in plain language.

Introduction

SPOŠLearning ("we", "us", "our") is a study platform for students at SPOŠ. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle personal data when you use our website (sposlearning.cz). We are committed to protecting your privacy and processing your data only where strictly necessary to run the site.

Because our users are primarily students in the European Union, we operate in compliance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). The data controller responsible for your data is the SPOŠLearning team, reachable at tadeasnosek@sposlearning.cz.

1 What Information We Collect

We believe in collecting only what is necessary to run SPOŠLearning. When you use our site, we may collect:

person

Account Information

Your display name, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password when you register for an account.

fingerprint

Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) Data

If you enable 2FA, we store the necessary data to verify your identity. This includes:

  • Passkeys: Public key credentials only. We never store or have access to your biometric data — that remains securely on your device.
  • TOTP (Authenticator Apps): A secure shared secret key.
  • Email OTP: We use your registered email address to send one-time codes.
network_ping

Log Data & IP Addresses

We temporarily log IP addresses for security purposes, such as preventing malicious attacks or spam. If you are logged in, your IP address is temporarily associated with your session to keep your account secure.

cookie

Cookies

We store small text files on your device to keep you logged in and remember your preferences. See Section 5 for full details.

policy
Legal basis (GDPR Art. 6): Processing is based on performance of a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)) — providing the account and authentication service you signed up for — and legitimate interests (Art. 6(1)(f)) for security logging.

2 How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect strictly to operate and maintain the site. Specifically:

  • check_circle To create and manage your account.
  • check_circle To allow you to log in securely and to support enhanced security features like Two-Factor Authentication (2FA).
  • check_circle To remember your display preferences (e.g. dark or light mode).
  • check_circle To protect the site from abuse, spam, or security threats.

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing or advertising purposes.

3 Third-Party Sign-In (Google & Microsoft)

You may sign in to SPOŠLearning using your existing Google or Microsoft account. When you choose to do so:

  • arrow_forward You are redirected to Google's or Microsoft's own sign-in page. We never see your Google or Microsoft password.
  • arrow_forward Upon successful authentication, the third-party provider shares limited profile information with us — typically your name and email address — which we use to create or identify your SPOŠLearning account.
  • arrow_forward We do not receive or store any other data from your Google or Microsoft account (e.g. contacts, calendar, files).

Your use of Google or Microsoft sign-in is also subject to their respective privacy policies:

open_in_new Google Privacy Policy open_in_new Microsoft Privacy Statement

4 How We Store and Protect Your Data

Your security is important to us. We take the following measures to protect your data:

  • lock Passwords are never stored in plain text. They are hashed using a strong, industry-standard algorithm before being stored.
  • lock Connections to our site are encrypted via HTTPS/TLS.
  • lock We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties.
schedule
Data retention: Security log data (including IP addresses) is retained only temporarily and is deleted once it is no longer needed for its security purpose. Account data is retained for as long as your account exists. Upon account deletion, all associated personal data is permanently removed.

5 Cookies

We use "functional" cookies solely to operate the site and improve your experience. We use cookies to:

  • check_circle Store a secure login token so you remain logged in as you navigate the site.
  • check_circle Remember your site appearance preferences (such as dark mode or layout choices).
Cookie Purpose Type
session_token Keeps you logged in securely Functional
spos_theme Remembers your light/dark mode preference Functional
spos_grade Remembers your selected grade level Functional
block
No tracking cookies. We do not use any third-party tracking, analytics, or advertising cookies. Because our cookies are strictly necessary for the site to function, they are set automatically when you use the site.

6 Your Rights (GDPR)

As a user in the European Union, you have the following rights under the GDPR regarding your personal data:

visibility
Right of Access

You can request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.

edit
Right to Rectification

You can correct inaccurate data (e.g. your display name) directly in your account settings.

delete
Right to Erasure

You can request the permanent deletion of your account and all associated data.

pause_circle
Right to Restriction

You can request that we restrict processing of your data in certain circumstances.

gavel
Right to Object

You can object to processing based on legitimate interests.

flag
Right to Lodge a Complaint

You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Czech supervisory authority, the Úřad pro ochranu osobních údajů (ÚOOÚ).

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at tadeasnosek@sposlearning.cz. We will respond within 30 days.

7 Account Management

You have direct control over your personal data through your account settings:

  • manage_accounts
    Updating your info: You can change your display name and manage your 2FA settings at any time directly in your account settings.
    Note: Currently, we do not support changing the email address associated with your account.
  • delete_forever
    Deleting your account: To permanently delete your account and all associated data, please email us at tadeasnosek@sposlearning.cz using the email address linked to your account. All personal data will be permanently removed.
